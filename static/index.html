Kisan Jal-Mitra bridges continental-scale satellite telemetry from the UN Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO WaPOR) with ground-level IoT moisture data. The portal translates complex remotely-sensed evapotranspiration and biomass observations into simple, field-level irrigation priorities to prevent crop failure while conserving critical groundwater resources.

⚠️ The Irrigation Paradox

Agriculture consumes 70% of freshwater. Over 60% of pumped irrigation is wasted through unguided calendar-based flooding, depleting aquifers and triggering root hypoxia without increasing crop yields.

🛰️ FAO WaPOR Telemetry

Every 10 days, FAO satellite observations track Actual Evapotranspiration (AETI), Precipitation (PCP), and Net Primary Production (NPP), measuring actual crop water consumption without manual surveys.

🌲 Champion Decision Tree

Our deployed ML engine (crop_stress_model.pkl) delivers 86.9% classification accuracy and 3.83 MAE, executing sub-millisecond inference for instant advisory slips.

4-Step Decision Support Pipeline

How satellite telemetric data is converted into actionable farmer guidance

Step 1 • Satellite Orbit
WaPOR Telemetry Ingestion

Captures 10-day evapotranspiration and dekadal precipitation across agricultural basins.

Step 2 • Field Telemetry
Root-Zone Sensor Feed

Measures current soil moisture percentage and ambient temperature against crop wilting thresholds.

Step 3 • ML Inference
Decision Tree Evaluation

Calculates stress risk score (0-100) and categorizes priority into 4 operational tiers.

Step 4 • Action Advisory
Irrigation Ticket Issued

Outputs recommended water depth (mm) and required irrigation timeframe for farmers.
