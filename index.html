1. The Crisis: The Irrigation Paradox

Agriculture consumes 70% of global freshwater. In developing basins, over 60% of pumped irrigation is wasted due to calendar-based flood irrigation, depleting groundwater tables and triggering root hypoxia.

🛰️

2. The Data: FAO WaPOR Satellite Telemetry

FAO's satellite portal delivers 10-day dekadal observations: Actual Evapotranspiration (AETI), Precipitation (PCP), and Net Primary Production (NPP), tracking crop vigor across vast regions without ground surveys.

🌲

3. The Engine: Champion Decision Tree

Our single serialized model (crop_stress_model.pkl) delivers 86.9% classification accuracy and an R² of 0.9495, outperforming complex ensembles with zero latency.
