1. The Crisis: The Irrigation Paradox

Agriculture consumes 70% of global freshwater. In developing agricultural basins, over 60% of pumped irrigation water is lost through unguided calendar-based flood irrigation, depleting aquifers and triggering root hypoxia.

🛰️

2. The Telemetry: FAO WaPOR Remote Sensing

By monitoring 10-day dekadal intervals, we assimilate Actual Evapotranspiration (AETI), Precipitation (PCP), and Net Primary Production (NPP) alongside root-zone soil moisture and ambient temperature.

🌲
